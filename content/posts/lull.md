When I see the date on the last post, it makes me sad. Blogging is fun, especially when you've got something interesting to say. Maybe something to teach or something to show. Something worth sharing. Sometimes you just don't feel like it. It's not writer's block, but a general lack of will to sit down and take the time to type out what you have to say. I have plenty of ideas for posts. Some are even partially written. I just can't. Won't. Don't. And I know blogging is not mandatory, but I wish I felt like it right now.

Ebbs and flows, I suppose. There are times when the writing just pours out of me and there's a deluge of posts. One after the other. I think I even had a day where I did two posts on the same day—though I am unwilling to check if that's true or a fantasy I decided to believe. Right now, I am not writing and I don't think I will for a little while. Well, I cobbled this together and it does feel good to be typing something _bloggy_, I must admit. Still, I'm gonna take the pause for now.

Anyway—that's all I have. Now let's see if I remember how to publish a blog post... after coffee.
